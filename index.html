An old bungalow, a garden, and the slow business of tea.

STG stands for Sabah Tea Garden, the Malaysian plantation in Borneo where our leaf is grown and packed. In Ipoh we pour it in a renovated 1937 bungalow at Chateau Garden, set back in its own garden with easy parking out front.

Inside, tables are spaced far enough apart that a conversation can wander. Come for a pot of tea and scones in the afternoon, or stay through dinner: oysters and escargots, charcoal pizza, crab pasta, and a properly made chicken rice.

“Tea at four, dinner at eight, and nobody hurrying you out in between.”
